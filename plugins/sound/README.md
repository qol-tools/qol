# Sound

Choose where sound plays and how loud.

</div>

## Quick start

```sh
qol-sound outputs
qol-sound switch "Living Room"
qol-sound volume 40
qol-sound doctor
```

Sound works from a terminal on its own. qol-tray is one way to use it, not a requirement.

## Hotkeys

- Next Sound Output switches to the next connected output and remembers it.
- Volume Up raises the volume of the output everything plays on by 5 percent.
- Volume Down lowers the volume of the output everything plays on by 5 percent.
