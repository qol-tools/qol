# Audio

Choose where sound plays, which microphone every app hears, and how loud each is.

</div>

## Quick start

```sh
qol-sound outputs
qol-sound switch "Living Room"
qol-sound volume 40
qol-sound inputs
qol-sound switch-input "Virtuoso"
qol-sound input-volume 80
qol-sound mute input
qol-sound levels
qol-sound doctor
```

Audio works from a terminal on its own. qol-tray is one way to use it, not a requirement.

## Settings

- Output picks where sound plays; Microphone picks what every app hears.
- Each Volume row lights with what is playing or being heard, up to the volume knob.
- Mute output and Mute microphone silence one side until you turn it back on.
- Microphone follows the output picks a headset's own microphone when you pick that headset for sound.

## Hotkeys

- Next Sound Output switches to the next connected output and remembers it.
- Volume Up raises the volume of the output everything plays on by 5 percent.
- Volume Down lowers the volume of the output everything plays on by 5 percent.
- Mute Output, Unmute Output, Mute Microphone and Unmute Microphone silence or restore one side.
